# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and a static portfolio-style app structure.

## Users

Primary users are potential clients, collaborators, and hiring managers evaluating Apanka Ayebadek David as a digital professional. They are looking for evidence of practical technical capability, domain relevance, and professional credibility in agriculture, data, and software delivery.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Apanka's work, expertise, and professional identity in a way that is credible, easy to scan, and aligned with his background in agricultural engineering, data, and full-stack product work. The site exists to help people understand what he does, what he has built, and how to contact him for work or collaboration.

Success means giving visitors a clear sense of his capabilities, domain focus, and trustworthiness in a short browsing session.

## Positioning

The product is a personal portfolio and service-facing web presence, positioned as a practical builder who connects agriculture, data, and technology to solve real problems. It is not a generic agency site or a broad personal brand landing page; it is grounded in a specific mix of engineering discipline, applied research, and digital execution.

## Operating Context

The site is used in standard web browsing environments, typically on desktop and mobile, where visitors assess professional fit quickly. Content and navigation are expected to support clear reading, short decision-making cycles, and direct access to contact or inquiry paths.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Show the portfolio owner’s professional identity and core disciplines.
- Highlight key roles and expertise: data and analytics, full-stack development, digital marketing, and agrictech.
- Provide navigation to key sections such as About, Projects, Services, Experience, and Contact.
- Use a brand visual language rooted in deep green, paper, and editorial typography.
- Must remain lightweight, fast, and easy to maintain in a small Next.js project.
- Current project scope is a personal portfolio rather than a full product SaaS or business application.

## Brand Commitments

The official identity is Apanka Ayebadek David. The tone is practical, confident, and professional, with a clear connection between technical capability and agricultural context. The project uses an earthy green palette and editorial styling to communicate grounded expertise, clarity, and credibility.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project name and scope confirmed in the workspace and package metadata.
- Site copy and header/footer content present in the app components and layout.
- Role descriptions and skill mapping are present in the role data file.
- Current visual direction includes a green-and-paper palette, Fraunces serif headings, and a tabbed professional-profile hero section.
- The app currently reflects a portfolio landing page with established brand language but without a complete long-form content set for all routes. Future work should avoid inventing full case studies, testimonials, or client claims that are not present in the repo.

## Product Principles

- Practical expertise over hype.
- Tie technical work back to real-world systems and outcomes.
- Keep the interface clear, credible, and easy to trust.
- Make the professional story legible in seconds.
- Root every digital expression in the agriculture-to-technology narrative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product should be readable, keyboard-friendly, and usable across common screen sizes with strong contrast. The current project already expresses a strong accessibility orientation through semantic structure and straightforward content hierarchy, and future design work should protect that clarity for diverse users and contexts.
